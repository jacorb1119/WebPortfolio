EMBEDDED SYSTEMS · IOT

UGV with Hazard Identification

The research goal was to design and set up a sensor system using E-Nose technology within a UGV for spotting chemical hazards. It sought to find a way to detect dangerous materials in risky locations and reduce the risk to those working there. Previously, human workers supervised devices at nuclear facilities which made accidents more likely and increased toxic exposure. The prototype was assembled by combining MQ2 and MQ137 gas sensors, a DHT22 sensor for measuring temperature and humidity and an ESP32 microcontroller.
